Meet the Workshop

Got a bag of loose parts and no idea what minifig they belong to? The Workshop is your space to figure it out — piece by piece, together.

1

Scan a part

Point your camera at any piece — a head, torso, or leg — and FigHub narrows down the candidates.

2

Build the picture

The more parts you scan, the clearer it gets. FigHub tracks what you have and what you still need.

3

Complete your minifig

When your minifig is complete, promote it to your main collection with one tap. 🎉
