Delete your FigHub account

You can delete your FigHub account and the data that goes with it at any time. FigHub is provided by FigHub Ltd.

In the app

  1. Open FigHub and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete My Data.
  3. Confirm. Your profile is hidden straight away and you're signed out.

By email

If you can't use the app, email hello@fighub.app from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We confirm requests through that address before we act.

What happens next

  • For 30 days you can change your mind. Sign in again and choose Recover.
  • After 30 days your account is erased. That includes your email address, date of birth, username, profile picture, collection, wishlist, Workshop builds, badges, your photos, your connections and your scan history.
  • Photo library copies are erased too, unless you chose to leave them with us. Photos you leave are no longer linked to you.

What we keep

  • Safety records, such as records of photo checks, reports, warnings and bans, with your account ID removed. A banned or reported username, and the text of a report, may stay in these records.
  • Promo code redemptions and technical logs, with your account ID removed.
  • Child-safety reports are kept for one year from the report, or longer if law enforcement asks. An account linked to one can't be deleted until then.
  • Anonymous usage stats, which were never linked to your account, are deleted after 13 months.

Things to know

  • Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first (Payments & subscriptions).
  • Want to delete some data but keep your account? You can remove figures, photos and your profile picture in the app, or email us and we'll help.
  • Using FigHub without an account? Your collection is stored only on your phone. Delete My Data removes your scan history from our servers, and uninstalling the app removes the rest.
