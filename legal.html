The short version

FigHub is an app for LEGO® minifigure collectors aged 13 and over. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it and what you can do about it.

Your photos

When you scan a figure or part

  1. The app shrinks the photo and removes location and camera details.
  2. The photo is checked for safety (see "Keeping FigHub safe").
  3. If it passes, it's sent to Brickognize, and to OpenAI if needed, to work out which figure it is.
  4. We don't keep it, unless one of the three cases below applies.

Brickognize, the service that recognises figures, keeps the scan photos it receives and uses them to improve its recognition, and may keep them indefinitely. It runs its own service and decides how it uses those photos, so we can't delete them for you. It receives only the photo (location and camera details removed), sent from our server, so it doesn't get your IP address or any account details. For the best results and the most privacy, keep faces and personal things out of the frame.

This happens whether or not you're signed in.

When we keep a photo

  • You save it as your own photo of a figure. It's stored privately. Only you can see it, unless you switch on "Show my photos to other collectors".
  • You've opted in to the photo library. See below.
  • The photo is refused by our safety checks. We keep a copy for up to 90 days so a person can review the decision, then delete it. If a photo is refused as possibly sexualising a child, a person at FigHub reviews it within 24 hours. If it's a false alarm, we delete it straight away. If not, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and keep it for one year from the report, or longer if law enforcement asks. Photos linked to any other child-safety report are kept for as long as the law requires. We keep the record of the check itself.

The photo library (optional, adults only)

If you're 18 or over, you can choose to let us keep copies of your scan photos to help improve identification in future. This is off unless you turn it on, at sign-up or in Settings.

  • Copies are stored without location details and are seen only by FigHub staff.
  • You can switch it off at any time. We'll ask whether you want us to keep the photos you've already shared or delete them.
  • If you delete your account, your copies are deleted too, unless you choose to leave them with us. Photos you leave are no longer linked to you.
  • It is never offered to under-18s.

Profile pictures are checked in the same way before they're saved. Anyone who can see your profile can see your profile picture.

Take care with backgrounds. A photo can show more than the figure: a face, a room, a letter on a desk. Photograph figures on a plain surface where you can.

Keeping FigHub safe

FigHub is used by teenagers as well as adults, so we check content before it's used. UK law (the Online Safety Act 2023) also requires us to do this.

Automatic checks on every photo and username

  • Known child sexual abuse material. A digital fingerprint of each photo (not the photo itself) is compared with a database of known illegal images, using Microsoft PhotoDNA.
  • Other harmful content. Each photo, and each username you choose, is checked by an automated moderation service run by OpenAI.
  • We keep a record of each photo check: the result, a fingerprint of the image, and your account ID if you're signed in. We don't keep photos that pass.

If a photo is refused

  • It isn't added, and the app tells you.
  • We keep a copy for up to 90 days so a person at FigHub can review it. An alert is emailed to our moderation team with a link to the image that expires after 7 days.
  • During those 90 days we may re-check the image automatically, to make sure our checks aren't refusing things by mistake. If a decision looks wrong, our team is told.

Reports from other collectors

Any signed-in collector can report a profile or a photo. Reports are read by FigHub staff, who can dismiss the report or warn, suspend or ban the account. An email alert goes to our moderation team with both usernames, the reason and comment, and, where relevant, a link to the picture that expires after 7 days. We don't tell the person reported who reported them. If a report is upheld against someone who is connected with an under-18, the account is banned.

Reporting child sexual abuse material

If a photo matches known child sexual abuse material, we are required to report it. We report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the United States, which passes reports to law enforcement, including in the UK. The report includes the account's email address, username and account ID, the IP address used for the upload, the time, and the image's fingerprint. We never store the image. We keep our record of the incident for one year after the report, or longer if law enforcement asks us to, and we can't delete the account until then.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings > Delete My Data. There is also a web page explaining how to delete your account.

  1. Your profile is hidden from everyone straight away, and you're signed out.
  2. For 30 days you can change your mind: sign in and choose Recover.
  3. After 30 days we erase your account: your email, date of birth, username, profile picture, collection, wishlist, Workshop builds, badges, photos, connections and scan history. Your photo library copies are erased too, unless you chose to leave them with us. We also ask RevenueCat to delete its record of you.

What stays: the safety records described above, with your account ID removed, for the times listed above; promo code redemptions and technical logs with your account ID removed; and records held by other companies under their own rules (for example, Google's record of your purchases).

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first.

If you use FigHub without an account, your collection is stored only on your phone. "Delete My Data" removes your scan history from our servers; uninstalling the app removes the rest.

You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing hello@fighub.app.
