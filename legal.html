2. Your Personal Minifigure Photos

This section is about the photos you choose to take of your own minifigures to build your personal collection. These are different from the scan photos used for AI identification, which are covered separately in Section 3.

In plain English:

  • Where they are stored. Personal photos you take of your minifigures are stored securely in Supabase Storage, our cloud storage provider.
  • What they are linked to. Each photo is linked to your personal collection record so it appears alongside the right minifigure in your collection.
  • Who can see them. If you make your profile public, or share your collection with people on your collector list, your personal minifigure photos may be visible to those other users. If your profile is private, only you can see them.
  • You control who sees them. You decide who can see your photos using your privacy settings. The options are Everyone, Collector list only, or Nobody.
  • You can delete them at any time. You can remove any personal photo whenever you like from the edit screen for that minifigure. Deleting a photo removes it from your collection record.

3. Scan Images and AI Identification

Please read this section carefully — it explains exactly what happens to the photos you take when you scan a minifigure, and how to opt out of AI training.

When you scan a minifigure using the FigHub app, here is what happens to that scan image, in plain English:

  • The photo is sent to OpenAI for identification. Your scan photo is sent to OpenAI's GPT-4o model, which identifies the minifigure for you. OpenAI processes the image as part of providing the identification result. OpenAI's handling of data is governed by their own privacy policy, available at https://openai.com/privacy.
  • An anonymised copy is kept to improve identification accuracy. FigHub keeps an anonymised copy of the scan image so we can improve the accuracy of our AI identification over time.
  • Anonymised images are not linked to you. Once a scan image has been anonymised, it is no longer connected to your account or any other personal data. It cannot be used to identify you.
  • You can opt out of AI training at any time. Go to Settings → Privacy → "Contribute to AI training" and turn the toggle off. When you opt out, your scan images are not retained or used to train or improve our AI.
  • Opting out does not stop you scanning. AI identification still works exactly the same way whether the toggle is on or off. Opting out only affects whether your scan images are kept to help improve the AI — it does not limit your ability to scan minifigures.

The Workshop feature also uses AI scanning. The same data handling described in this section applies to Workshop scans as well as standard minifigure scans.

4. Other Data We Collect

For each confirmed scan we collect:

  • The minifigure ID (BrickLink and/or Rebrickable format)
  • The AI-generated identification result and confidence score

4.2 Collection Data

We store your personal collection of minifigures, including any personal details you add such as notes, condition, purchase price, and purchase date. If you are a registered user, your collection may also be synced to our servers.

4.3 Usage and Analytics Data

We collect usage data including:

7. Your Collector List

FigHub lets you connect with other collectors. In plain English:

  • You can add other collectors to your collector list. This lets you share parts of your collection with people you choose.
  • Collectors on your list may see your collection, profile, and collection value — but only to the extent allowed by your privacy settings.
  • You control exactly what is visible. Your privacy settings decide what people on your collector list can and cannot see.
  • You can remove a collector at any time. Removing someone from your collector list is immediate, and it instantly revokes their access to the data you had been sharing with them.

8. Public Profiles

10. Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Scan images and identification data — for users who have not opted out of AI training, anonymised scan images may be retained as part of our training dataset (see Sections 3 and 11). Identification data is retained for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy.
  • Your personal collection data — retained for as long as you use the app, and deleted on the schedule set out in Section 11 when you delete your account.
  • Analytics data — retained for as long as commercially useful, typically up to 36 months for identifiable data.
  • Subscription data — retained as required by applicable law and accounting obligations.

11. Account Deletion and Soft Delete

If you delete your FigHub account, in plain English:

  • Your personal data is kept for 30 days first. When you delete your account, we do not erase your personal data immediately. We retain it for 30 days.
  • This 30-day grace period lets us recover accidentally deleted accounts. If you deleted your account by mistake, this window gives us the chance to restore it.
  • After 30 days, your personal data is permanently and irreversibly deleted. Once the grace period ends, your personal data cannot be recovered.
  • Anonymised scan images are not deleted. Anonymised scan images that have been retained for AI training contain no personal data and are not linked to you, so they are not removed as part of account deletion. (You can prevent your scan images from being retained at all by opting out of AI training — see Section 3.)
