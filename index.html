A bag of loose parts? Build it back up

The Workshop works out which minifigure your parts belong to, then tracks what you still need to finish it.

  1. 1Scan a part

    Photograph a torso, head or helmet. FigHub identifies the piece and lists the figures that use it.

  2. 2Narrow it down

    Scan another part from the same figure and the list shrinks, here from five figures to one.

  3. 3Tick off what you have

    See every part the figure needs, mark the ones you own and find the rest.

  4. 4Add it to your collection

    Once every part is ticked, the finished figure joins your collection in one tap.
